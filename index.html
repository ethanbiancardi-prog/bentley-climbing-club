Your route to the top

  1. 1

    Join the club

    Sign up on Campus Groups with your Bentley login, then hop in the GroupMe. That's where you'll hear about trips first.

    Join on Campus Groups Join our GroupMe
  2. 2

    Do your CRG forms

    Central Rock Gym needs these before you can climb. They're quick, you don't need an account, and they work at every Central Rock location.

    Participation AgreementThe main waiver Required Facility & Bouldering OrientationFor all adult climbers Required Auto Belay OrientationIf you'll climb on auto belays If needed Top Rope OrientationIf you'll top rope or belay. Belayers also pass a staff test. If needed

    Under 18? A parent or legal guardian has to complete your forms, so sort that out early. Not sure what you need? See CRG's required forms page.

  3. 3

    Come climb

    Trips have limited spots, so sign up on Campus Groups when one is posted. Each listing has the time, the cost and whether rides are provided. At the gym, tell the front desk you already signed online.

First time?

  • No gear needed. It's available at the gym, and staff will fit you at check-in.
  • Wear comfortable clothes you can move freely in.
  • No experience needed. Club leaders are there to teach you. See CRG's first-timer guide for more.

The club

The Bentley Climbing Club brings students together to go rock climbing in a positive, team-building environment. It's a chance to build leadership and problem-solving skills, stay active, and blow off steam with people who share the same interest.
