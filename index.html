Fill out your CRG forms

You do these on Central Rock Gym's website, and each one takes you straight to the form. Do them once and they count at every Central Rock location.

Everyone needs these two

Participation Agreement The main waiver. Required to check in, even if you're only watching. Open form → Facility & Bouldering Orientation Required for all adult climbers and chaperones. Open form →

Only if you'll use these

Auto Belay Orientation For climbing on auto belays. Open form → Top Rope Orientation For top roping or belaying. Belayers also pass a staff belay test. Open form →
  • At the gym: tell the front desk you already signed online.
  • Under 18? A parent or legal guardian has to complete your forms, so sort this out before the event.
  • Not sure what you need? See CRG's full required forms page, or email us.

Your first time

You don't need experience or your own gear. Club leaders and our faculty advisor offer training and advice to all members.

  • Wear comfortable clothes you can move freely in.
  • Gear is available at the gym, and staff will fit you at check-in.
  • Check the event listing for the gym, time and any cost.
  • Read CRG's first-time climbers guide if you want more detail.

About the club

The Bentley Climbing Club brings students together to go rock climbing in a positive, team-building environment. It's a chance to build leadership and problem-solving skills, stay active, and blow off steam with people who share the same interest.

Climbing is a community sport that brings together people of all backgrounds, and that's how we run the club.
